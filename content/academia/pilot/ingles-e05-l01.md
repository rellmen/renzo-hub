# Writing a strong 40–60 word answer

**Concept.** The exam evaluates accuracy and whether you answer the precise reading question. Build a four-part response: direct answer → evidence from the text → explanation → final sentence. Avoid adding unsupported details. With 40–60 words there is no room for a long introduction.

**Prompt based on E03 text:** «Why was training still important after the app was introduced?»

**Model answer (approximately 45 words):** «Training was still important because employees sometimes faced situations that the normal procedure did not explain. Although the app reduced mistakes, staff had to understand how to handle unusual cases. This shows that introducing a digital tool does not replace the need for human judgement.»

**Editing checklist:** 40–60 words, correct tenses, subject-verb agreement, no copied paragraph, supported claim, spelling. **Practice:** write a different 40–60 word answer with the same meaning; compare to checklist rather than memorizing model.
