# Present Simple vs Present Continuous

**Concept.** Use **present simple** for routines, habits and general facts: «I check stock every morning». Use **present continuous** (`am/is/are + verb-ing`) for actions happening now or temporary situations: «I am checking a delivery right now».

**Example 1:** «Every Friday, the team updates the report». It is a repeated action, so present simple.

**Example 2:** «Look! The robot is moving the pallet». The action is taking place now, so present continuous.

**Common mistake:** «I am work every day» blends the forms. Use «I work every day» or «I am working now».

**Practice:** A) Right now, I ___ (study) English. B) We usually ___ (finish) at five. C) Today she ___ (work) from home.
**Answers:** A) am studying B) finish C) is working.
